## Read access

The JARVIS materials linked here are already in this public repository. Outreach recipients and other readers can open them without a collaborator invitation. This update adds a reading guide; it does not grant write, maintain, or administrator privileges, change repository visibility, or publish private archives.

The routes below organize relevance, not permissions: every public reader can see all public routes. They do not restrict access to individual folders.

## Adaptive routing rule

Choose one or more routes from a recipient's voluntarily stated interests or verified professional remit. Combine matching public links without duplicates. When the remit is unknown, use R10. Do not infer private beliefs, health, relationships, or preferences from music, imagery, likes, or followers. New reviewed public material can be added to this index; routing is currently manual, not a running agent.

All outreach readers may use this index. Private-review requests require separately identified recipients and explicitly approved files. Reading access does not imply review, agreement, partnership, or endorsement.

## What is not in this release

No raw correspondence, recipient-address lists, medical notes, personal photographs, private conversations, credentials, or unpublished research archives are uploaded by this update. Repository-wide confidentiality has not been certified by creating an index. A sensitive file must not be placed here merely under a folder named private.

The original research documents and earlier policy files are not replaced. Existing evidence and consent requirements remain in force. This guide does not change existing licenses or ownership notices.

## Access and delivery are separate

`PUBLICLY_READABLE` is not `SENT`, `DELIVERED`, `READ_BY_RECIPIENT`, or `ENDORSED`. This index and CSV do not send email, invite collaborators, activate agents, monitor accounts, or establish continuous project protection. No individual recipient's access or reading has been tested by this publication.

For a later restricted collaboration, organization-owned repositories support a Read role. Personal private-repository collaborator access must not be assumed to be read-only. Administrative changes require an authorized supported action and verification after the change.
