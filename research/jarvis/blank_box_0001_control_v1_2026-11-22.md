## If a probability interpretation is later explicitly established
If `p=0.0001` refers to an independently sampled Bernoulli failure probability, the numeric value is 0.01% or 1 in 10,000. Under i.i.d. trials and zero observed failures, a one-sided 95% upper confidence bound of 0.0001 needs
`n >= ceil(ln(0.05)/ln(1-0.0001)) = 29,956` independent observations.
This is not a claim that a JARVIS model attained that bound; correlated failures, distribution shift, adaptive testing, adversarial behaviors and unidentified failure modes break or limit its applicability. Earlier aspirational bounds such as `10^-6` are not silently overwritten.
