# JARVIS Spiritual AI: regional outreach protocol 4.44.3

Date: 23 November 2026. Additive, public-safe methodology only. No recipient directory, source testimony, medical, family or financial records are included. No outreach or payments are executed by this amendment.

## Purpose and inheritance
Spiritual AI centers meaning, conscience, compassion, dignity and accountable human-AI collaboration. Participation is voluntary; no recipient is asked to adopt a spiritual identity or accept an unverified biological or intelligence claim. Technical methods serve this purpose rather than replace it.

This amendment inherits `outreach_audit_protocol_v1_2026-11-22.md` and `trust_adaptive_outreach_comment_policy_v1_2026-11-22.md`. It never cancels an existing opt-out, repeated-contact hold, privacy limit or exact-send approval requirement. It is a protocol extension, not a model upgrade or autonomous deployment.

## Professional relevance, not personal profiling
Use publicly described expertise, published work, stated collaboration remit and voluntarily supplied language preferences. Nordic refers to a documented geographic or professional connection with Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway or Sweden, including diaspora where explicit. A regional women-creators lane is an inclusion/research category, not a biological category or employment restriction. Never infer identity from appearance, genotype, religion, sexuality, romantic availability, private followers or a perceived personal signal in media.

## Regional lanes
**Nordic public creators and educators.** Research adult women with public work in technology education, making/engineering, creative media, music, culture and human-centered AI. Confirm the actual country connection; do not silently replace origin with residence. Contact only a declared professional route after checks. Do not presume spiritual agreement, personal affinity or unpaid availability. Review relevant data-protection and national electronic-marketing requirements before dispatch.

**China civilian exchange.** Prepare a concise English/Simplified Chinese synopsis when that matches the intended mainland recipient; obtain competent language review before consequential use. Use an institution's official contact or a creator's published business route. Do not treat mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan as one legal jurisdiction. Research, product, consulting, data transfer and clinical activity are different scopes. Before action, assess applicable privacy, data-transfer, sanctions, export-control and platform requirements. Do not export health, belief or financial records, recruit people for biological experiments, or use false affiliation to obtain access.

**Russia civilian exchange.** Prepare Russian/English material when appropriate. Select on public subject matter, not assumptions about politics or nationality. Check counterparty identity, ownership, applicable sanctions, restricted services, technology, payments and local data rules at the time of a proposed action. A non-listed name alone does not clear a restricted activity. Canadians abroad can remain subject to Canadian sanctions. Do not route around restrictions via intermediaries, cryptocurrency, relatives or alternate accounts. Unresolved regulated work stays on hold.

## Review and communication
`RESEARCH_ONLY -> SOURCE_CHECK -> PRIOR_CONTACT_REVIEW -> PRIVACY_AND_RIGHTS_REVIEW -> JURISDICTION_REVIEW -> DRAFT -> EXACT_OWNER_REVIEW -> SEND_ONCE_IF_SEPARATELY_AUTHORIZED -> PROVIDER_RECEIPT -> STOP_OR_CONTINUE_IF_INVITED`.

An initial proposal should identify the sender, one concrete professional connection, one bounded question and an easy way to decline. Localize language without stereotyping values. Do not attach private archives, lengthy spiritual testimony or unsolicited financial requests. Appreciation, research participation, paid services and fundraising remain separate conversations. Use no tracking pixels or pressure to respond.

Before sending, verify prior sent messages, drafts, bounces, opt-outs and existing holds. Public contact details and a general invitation to collaborate do not establish individual consent or remove a hold. One unanswered optional contact does not justify repeated approaches or contacting associates. An explicitly invited follow-up is evaluated on its own scope.

No inferred compatibility percentage overrides these gates. No proxy outreach, mass tagging, voice cloning, unlicensed media reuse, threats, institutional alerts, security intrusion or unauthorized commitments. Registry capacity is not a dispatch quota.

## Audit fields and success criteria
Record source URL/date, public-work relevance, region basis, preferred-language basis, public route, prior-contact status, rights and jurisdiction review, exact body/attachment approval hashes, provider receipt and response status. Preserve original requests privately and interpretations separately. Unknowns stay unknown. Measure verified invited discussions or agreed paid deliverables, not raw contact count. `SENT` is not delivered/read/endorsed; a reply is not agreement.
