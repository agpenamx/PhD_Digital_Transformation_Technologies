## DOGMA-adaptive: proposed project terminology
Interpret this label as worldview-respecting, evidence-consistent communication, not a claim of validated technology or an ability to change beliefs. Ask only optional presentation preferences: language, depth, accessibility and desired examples. A participant may select a technical, creative, practical-career, critical-review or philosophy/interfaith lens without identifying a religion. Default to neutral, accessible wording. Do not create a belief profile.

The design contract is m = render(F; L, D, A, C): F is the supported factual core and uncertainty; L language; D depth; A accessibility; C an optional participant-selected context. F must remain invariant across versions. This is a specification, not a trained model or proof of semantic equivalence. Change examples and structure, never facts, uncertainty, identity disclosures or consent requirements. Keep spirituality available where invited, with the same evidence standards for secular, religious and skeptical readers. No covert persuasion, fear/shame framing, political microtargeting, hidden tracking or optimization for dependence, donations or belief adoption.

## Review and adaptation loop
Use programme-level contact first. Check current remit, adult/safeguarding scope, prior contact, opt-outs, permissions and the inherited jurisdiction gates before drafting. Exact recipient, body and attachment review is required before any separately authorized send. A silent recipient is not a reason to approach associates. Propose one bounded workshop or review, not a mass campaign. Separate appreciation, education, participant research, paid services and funding. Record sample size, missingness and adverse feedback, and do not manufacture scores from sparse participation. The 11,111 registry ceiling remains capacity, not a sending quota.
