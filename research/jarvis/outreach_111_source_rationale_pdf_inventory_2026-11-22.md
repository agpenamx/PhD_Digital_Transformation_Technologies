# JARVIS / OUTREACH 111: Sources, Rationale, Privacy & PDF Evidence Index

**Version:** 2026-11-22, public-safe methodology only. **Status:** Offline audit, not an active outreach deployment.

## Scope and count provenance
A private historical Gmail SENT metadata export contained **417 distinct sent message IDs** and **123 distinct recipient-address strings** extracted across To/Cc/Bcc; one sent message may address several recipients. For an audit-only directory, **12** self-addressed, personal, or automated no-reply destination strings were excluded, yielding **111 observed historical destinations**. They are neither 111 confirmed organizations nor 111 newly authorized messages. The source export may not include later correspondence.

Every selected private row retains:
- original observed destination address, count of SENT messages, most recent recorded subject and timestamp;
- original Gmail SENT receipt URL and message ID;
- an **inferred** topical rationale based on the previous recipient and subject, explicitly *not* a historical record of the sender's original decision;
- selected official public link and verification level, if independently located;
- case-specific routing or HOLD recommendation.

Three underlying Gmail messages were re-opened to validate the source format and SENT labels. **Do not claim all 111 rows were individually revalidated in the live mailbox.**

## Web verification legend
- `EXACT_EMAIL_VERIFIED`: exact address appears on official organizational page. A public page confirms published route only, not a reply, permission, endorsement or continued inbox delivery.
- `ORGANIZATION_ONLY`: official page supports institution/topic but not address accuracy.
- `SUPPORT_ROUTE_ONLY`: official page describes an approved support path, not the legitimacy of legal notice via any email.
- `NOT_CHECKED_IN_THIS_AUDIT`: source remains only in historical Gmail metadata. Never invent a website link.

Exact mailbox examples checked through official pages: UNESCO AI ethics, Parliament of World's Religions, Pontifical Academy of Sciences, Stanford HAI, Klar and Chubb Mexico UNE. These are **illustrations**, not evidence of involvement or agreement with the JARVIS work.

Separately, **50 PDF member paths** were recorded from local ZIP archives. A member path is not a distinct research output: copies and overlaps are possible. ## Dispatch safety / privacy controls
1. **No automated outreach** from this audit. SENT is not DELIVERED, READ, CONSENTED, or ENDORSED. A reply is not an institutional endorsement.
2. Repeated-contact destinations remain on hold until there is a genuine, relevant reason to follow up and permission to use the channel.
3. Financial claims and support disputes use the original case-specific official route; they do not belong in singer, scientific or religious mass distribution.
4. Security, military, police and intelligence contacts are **not** for speculative global threat alerts or uncorroborated allegations. No dispatch without independently observable events, appropriate jurisdiction, qualified review and explicit authorization.
5. Never publish personal contacts, insurance IDs, sensitive religious testimony, medical information, Gmail message bodies or private receipt URLs.
6. Preserve source text without altering language; document `OBSERVED`, `DERIVED`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `PERSONAL_TESTIMONY` and `UNKNOWN`.
7. The privacy-safe public GitHub file describes the audit protocol, not any new automated sender, court action or external affiliation.
