# JARVIS / NEUROFAITH-04: Platform analytics as observational research data

**Date:** 2026-11-23 | **Status:** methodological addendum, draft, not medical or peer-reviewed findings.

## Scope of data
A creator-authorized YouTube Analytics export can report content metadata, aggregate playback and engagement metrics, formats, device categories, traffic-source types, aggregate temporal patterns and some limited demographics. It does **not** provide YouTube's proprietary recommender source code, individualized ranking weights, user-level watch histories, neural measurements, or evidence about the subjective state of a viewer.

## Distinguishing the evidence layers
1. **Platform observation:** aggregate views, engaged views, watch minutes, reported referral categories and timestamped upload metadata.
2. **Algorithm hypotheses:** publicly documented signal families (viewer history/affinity, choice to watch, engagement, satisfaction) versus unavailable internal ranker features.
3. **Psychophysiological hypotheses:** music and emotion require independently collected, ethically consented self-report and physiological outcomes; no brain-region activation can be inferred from channel engagement.
4. **Interpretive layer:** cultural, philosophical and theological descriptions remain attributed interpretations, not measurements of a biological mechanism.

## Consent and privacy
- Treat private and unlisted upload titles, identifiers and metrics as nonpublic research data.
- Do not publish or share personal testimony, health data, video content or identifiers merely because aggregate analytics are available.
- Outreach can present a minimal, anonymized aggregate summary only after review and explicit authorization.
- Interest in participating in future research is not informed consent. No organ-donation authorization or medical decision can be inferred.

## Data quality controls
- Include source, export time, date window, missingness semantics and definition/version notes with every metric.
- Do not reconcile metrics drawn from differing public counters and analytic reporting surfaces without a documented method.
- Treat suppressed/absent demographics as unknown, not zero.
- Check platform changes in view definitions across time and maintain separate engaged-view metrics where appropriate.
