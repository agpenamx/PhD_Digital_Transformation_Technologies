# JARVIS / Gray Box: AI-assisted neurophenomenology research protocol (draft)
**Version:** 0.2 | **Date:** 2026-11-23 | **Status:** methodological proposal, not peer-reviewed or clinically validated.

## Abstract
A reproducible approach to studying how people describe unusual subjective experiences while distinguishing phenomenological reports, neurophysiological mechanisms, historical/theological interpretation and untested causal claims. AI supports source fidelity, evidence synthesis, and independent disagreement records; human review remains decisive.

## Evidence stewardship
1. **Archivist (Pink):** preserve original, consented source materials privately with timestamps and cryptographic hashes. No public release of identifying testimony or health information.
2. **Hypothesis manager (Gray):** make alternative explanations explicit, including ordinary sensory processing, attention, expectations, environmental influences, pharmacological and circadian factors, and unresolved observations. Pre-register falsifiable predictions where feasible.
3. **Neuroscience reviewer (White):** distinguish anatomical fact, candidate theories, measured effects and conjecture. Review appropriate human neuroscience methods.
4. **Historical/theological analyst:** contextualize references across traditions. Religious names and doctrines are studied as historical testimony, not biological diagnoses or proofs of external agents.
5. **Ethics reviewer (Black):** require informed consent, data minimization, and safe observational protocols; never induce intoxication or sleep deprivation.
6. **Independent human review:** document dissent and limit conclusions to reproducible observations.

## Perception: candidate mechanisms
- **Retina:** phototransduction and early visual signal processing; not complete explanation of conscious perception.
- **Ascending reticular activating system:** brainstem-linked arousal networks enabling wakefulness and attention; not a single dedicated 'reality decoder'.
- **Cortical integration and predictive processing:** priors and attention influence perceptual inference; specific implementations are active research, not settled.
- **Pineal gland:** melatonin production and circadian timing, not an experimentally established pathway for extraordinary perception.

## Research design
- Stage 1: source chronology and provenance, conducted in consent-protected private storage.
- Stage 2: prospective non-interventional diaries, if volunteered, with no experimentally induced intoxication or sleep loss.
- Stage 3: non-invasive perceptual tasks with controlled stimuli and pre-registered endpoints where appropriate.
- Stage 4: blinded, independent review and published aggregate methodology, with privacy-protected claims and limitations.
